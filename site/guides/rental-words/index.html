Guide

Words in your Polish rental contract

Eight words you will meet in ads and contracts, each explained once.

Team review of these definitions confirm

czynsz
Rent: in ads it usually means what you pay the landlord each month, so check whether the building charge is included.
czynsz administracyjny
The building charge. It comes on top of the rent, and the price we show you includes it.
kaucja
The deposit, usually one month’s rent, paid once and returned when you move out, minus any documented damage.
umowa najmu
The rental contract between you and the landlord: check the dates, the notice period and what happens to the deposit.
media
Utilities, such as electricity, gas, water, heating and internet: check which are in the building charge and which you pay separately.
protokół zdawczo-odbiorczy
The handover record: the flat’s condition, meter readings and photos, signed when you move in and again when you move out.
najem okazjonalny
A type of rental contract that gives the landlord extra protection; it usually needs a notarised statement from you and an address you could move to.
odstępne
A one-off payment to take over a flat or its furniture from someone else: ask what exactly you are paying for.

Source Czynsz administracyjny and kaucja: IBB PAS Welcome Center, accommodation guide. The other words: general guidance, not legal advice.
