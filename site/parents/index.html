The contract, in plain English

Before anyone signs, we walk you and your child through the contract in plain English. The landlord side is handled in Polish.

We’re not lawyers and don’t give legal advice.

The deposit

The deposit (kaucja) is usually one month’s rent. It is paid once, and the handover record with photos documents the flat on the first day.

Source Deposit size: Kozminski University, student living costs (May 2026).

The full cost

Rent, the building charge (czynsz administracyjny) and utilities as one monthly number, and the deposit on its own line.

    Before you ask

    Are you lawyers?

    No. We walk you and your child through the contract in plain English, but we don’t give legal advice.

    What doesn’t the fee cover?

    Rent, the deposit (usually one month’s rent), legal advice and the move itself.

    Can I pay for my child’s search?

    Yes. You can pay for your child's search; the contract can still be in your child's name. confirm

    Which languages do you speak?

    Polish, Ukrainian, Macedonian and German; English as our working language confirm. With the landlord, we speak Polish.
