Your rules, shown correctly / Tenants who arrive briefed

Your rules, shown correctly

Before signing, we show every tenant your house rules. confirm

We present your building as it is: the rooms, the full monthly cost and the rules you give us.

Tenants who arrive briefed

Before signing, we walk every tenant through the contract in plain English. At move-in, the handover is documented with photos.

Fewer questions land on your team in the first week.

What we need from you

  • Room or flat details, with the full monthly cost
  • Your house rules
  • Access for viewings, in person or on live video
  • A written contract with every tenant

We do not work with landlords who will not let us see the flat or will not sign a written contract.
