About us

We built the service we would have paid for.

Two months before this semester, one of us (FILL IN: name of the founder whose flat was cancelled) had a flat in Warsaw confirmed. It was cancelled at short notice, leaving two weeks to find a new one from abroad. The new room turned up by chance, in a newly opened student residence, after days of stress.

The rest of us had versions of the same story: ads only in Polish, listings that vanished, and nobody to ask whether an offer was genuine.

Doorstep was founded in autumn 2026 by six students at Lazarski University: three on Erasmus exchange, three in full-time degree programmes. We are not trading yet.

Four things we don't bend on

  1. 01

    A named person on every case

    Software can sort offers. A person checks the flat and answers you.

  2. 02

    Nothing paid before it is checked

    We never ask you to send money for a flat no one has verified.

  3. 03

    We say who pays us

    If a landlord or residence pays us a fee for your placement, you see it before you decide.

  4. 04

    A fixed fee, not a share of your rent

    Our fee does not rise when your rent does, so we have no reason to push you higher.

Lazarski University

We met as students at Lazarski University in Warsaw. Doorstep is our own business, not a university service.

Who we’re not for

  • People who need a bed for less than a month
  • People who want the cheapest possible offer and will skip the checking
  • Companies with their own relocation department
  • Landlords who won’t let us see the flat or won’t sign a written contract
