Contracts shorter than 12 months

Many landlords want 12 months. We search for stays of 3 to 12 months and give your dates in the first message.

Landlords who accept foreigners

Viewings clash with work. One of us goes, films the flat and sends you the video, so you don’t leave the office.

You decide from the video, or come along after work if you’d rather see it yourself.

    Before you ask

    What’s the shortest stay?

    Three months. We don’t do stays under a month.

    Are you a relocation firm?

    No. If your company has a relocation department, you don’t need us. We help people whose employer doesn’t offer relocation support.

    Do you give legal advice?

    No. We walk you through the contract in plain English before you sign.

    Which languages do you speak?

    Polish, Ukrainian, Macedonian and German; English as our working language confirm. With the landlord, we speak Polish.
