Who pays us

You pay our fixed search fee. Universities in our partner programme pay an annual fee.

Some residences and landlords pay us a referral fee when a tenancy is signed: 50% of one month's rent per signed tenancy confirm. If that applies to a room on your shortlist, you see it before you decide.

A room is offered because it fits, not because it pays.

What we are not

  • Not a listings portal

    You get three to five checked options, not three thousand unchecked ones.

  • Not a percentage agency

    Our fee does not follow the rent.

  • Not lawyers

    We walk you through the contract in plain English. We don't give legal advice.

  • Not instant

    A checked shortlist takes days. We won't book an unseen flat tonight, and we don't apologise for that.

  • Not for stays under a month

    Short stays belong to hotels and holiday lets.

  • Not a relocation firm for companies

    Companies with their own relocation department don't need us.

Questions about how it works

When do I pay?

Paid when you sign confirm. No home, no fee. confirm

Is the deposit part of your fee?

No. The deposit (kaucja) goes to the landlord and is usually one month’s rent. Our fee is separate.

How long does it take?

A checked shortlist takes days. Searches start up to 8 weeks before you arrive. We won’t book an unseen flat tonight.

What if I can’t come to a viewing?

One of us views the flat in person or checks it live on video, and you get the video.

Do you work outside Warsaw?

No. We work in Warsaw only, so every viewing in the city can be done in person.

What’s the shortest stay?

4 months for students, 3 months for professionals. We don’t do stays under a month.

Tell us your dates, budget and campus.

We’ll come back with three to five options. Searches start up to 8 weeks before you arrive.
